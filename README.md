# tianas-tech-odyssey

Welcome to my personal tech journal 

I am a Bachelor's student in **Computer and Software Engineering at EFREI Paris**. I am deeply passionate about bridging the gap between hardware, software, and human interaction. As a self-taught electronics enthusiast, a FabLab instructor, and a science communicator, this repository is my living laboratory and digital journal.

My ultimate goal? Becoming a **Research Software Engineer (RSE)** by 2028 and creating smart, interactive tools that merge technology with creativity.

---

## What’s inside this journal ? 

I have structured this notebook into four distinct parts, mapping my journey from zero to building my own interactive systems:

* **🔬 [01. Components Dictionary](./01-components-dictionary)**
  * *What is it?* My personal encyclopedia of hardware components. 
  * *The Twist:* It includes standard definitions, technical schematics, and my raw, curiosity-driven questions (like *"What exactly is cold in a resistor?"*).

* **💡 [02. Practical Exercises](./02-practical-exercises)**
  * *What is it?* Step-by-step documentation of my hands-on electronic labs.
  * *The Twist:* Every project contains a **Fail Log**. I document my bugs, my burnt LEDs, and how I debugged them because learning by failing is the best way to grow.

* **🔍 [03. Cabinet of Curiosities](./03-cabinet-of-curiosities)**
  * *What is it?* Pure reverse engineering! I disassemble everyday objects to understand how they work from the inside out.
  * *Latest breakdown:* Dissecting a CD player and exploring the physical differences between CDs, DVDs, and Blu-Rays.

* **🎨 [04. Creative Projects](./04-creative-projects)**
  * *What is it?* Where engineering meets art, design, and textile innovation.
  * *Featuring:* My touch-sensitive smart lamp and my upcoming explorations into e-textiles and wearable tech.
